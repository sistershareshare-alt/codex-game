# Mandarin pronunciation

The word bank stores context-specific pinyin separately from stroke templates. In particular, 校长 = xiào zhǎng, 长尺 = cháng chǐ, 教室 = jiào shì and 快乐 = kuài lè. 长尺 has the invisible speech cue 常尺 to prevent speech engines from choosing zhǎng for 长. The displayed answer remains 长尺.

The voice selector prefers zh-CN / zh-SG Mandarin, then other explicitly Mandarin tags, and does not select zh-HK as a generic Chinese voice. Actual voice quality and availability depend on the tablet's installed speech engine; synthetic output cannot be guaranteed identical on every device.
